Specialized analysis tools for fibroblast cell populations and their functional states

🧬 Fibroblast Markers

Identification: COL1A1, PDGFRA
Subtypes: CLIC5 (lining), THY1 (sub-lining), CD34 (protective), CYBB (inflammatory)

🔬 Key Findings

CD34+ Loss: 54.49% reduced in obese OA
Pathways: Hypoxia (HIF1α), Adipogenesis (PPARG), ECM degradation (MMP3/13)
